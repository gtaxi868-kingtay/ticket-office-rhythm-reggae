Everything the big platforms do — priced for the events they ignore

Built for the 50-to-500-guest middle: fundraisers, school and church events, club fetes, boat rides, comedy and dance shows.

A real checkout

Buyers pick tickets and add-ons with steppers, answer your questions, and see the full total before they commit — then pay by transfer, card link, and attach their receipt.

Payment plans

Deposit today, balance later — tracked per order, so a TT$1,200 all-inclusive doesn't need TT$1,200 today. No card required.

Who sold what

Every seller signs in with their own PIN. Every ticket carries their name. The cash-up sheet shows what each person owes you, ready to print.

A door that can't be fooled

Codes are generated by the system and verified against a secret only the server holds. A used ticket says ALREADY USED — with the time and the name of who let it in first. Unpaid tickets are flagged on screen.

Transfers done safely

A buyer can't come? Transfer the ticket to the new holder — the old code dies and a fresh one is issued. Screenshots can't get two people in.

Break-even, live
