Install on iPhone

  1. Open this link in Safari (not inside WhatsApp).
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow pointing up).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add. The Tickets icon is now on your home screen.
  5. Open it from the icon — it works full-screen and opens even with no signal after the first load.

Android

  1. Open the link in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu → Add to Home screen (or “Install app”).

How your team uses this

  • Owner — sets up events, ticket types and the team; sees every sale live; does the cash-up.
  • Sellers — sign in with their own PIN and sell. Every ticket they issue appears on the owner's phone at once, under their name. Codes are issued centrally, so two sellers can never sell the same ticket number.
  • Door — sign in and scan each ticket's QR (or type the code + verify number). A ticket can only be let in once, on any phone — the second scan says ALREADY USED.
  • Forgotten PIN? — sellers ask the owner to reset it in Settings. The owner uses the recovery code shown once at setup.
  • No signal? — the app opens offline, but sales and check-ins need a connection to reach the shared system. Keep one phone on data at the door.
